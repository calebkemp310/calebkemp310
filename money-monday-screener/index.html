The four checks

News catalystfresh PR / 8-K / contract

Real company news since Friday. No PR means no trade, even if it is running.

Unusual volumeRVOL ≥ 5× or float turned ≥ 2×

Volume against the 10-day average, or shares traded divided by float.

Low float≤ 10M shares · A+ ≤ 1M

Low supply makes the spike. WORX ran 210% on a 741k float.

Price range$1 – $20 · up ≥ 10%

Under $1 is a sub-penny trap. A low price alone never earns a spot.

Build it in Webull

  1. Open Webull, tap Markets, then Screeners (desktop: Screener in the left bar). Start a new custom screener.
  2. Add the filters below, then save it as Money Monday.
  3. From 4:00 ET Monday, sort the results by % Change and cross-check with Top Gainers → Pre-market.
  4. Open the news tab on every name. No fresh PR, drop it.
Price$1.00 – $20.00

Filter names differ a little between the phone app and desktop. If your version has no float filter, use market cap as the stand-in and check float on each ticker's quote page.

Monday morning routine

  1. 4:00 Run the screener. Note every gapper ≥ 10% with a PR.
  2. 7:00 Re-run. Keep names whose premarket volume is still building. Drop anything fading back to Friday's close.
  3. 8:30 Mark levels on the top 2–3: premarket high, Friday's high, VWAP.
  4. 9:30 Let the opening spike go. Look for the first pullback that holds VWAP.
  5. Exit Stop just under VWAP or the pullback low. Lose VWAP, get out.
  6. After close Log the trade in your Trading Journal agent.

FOMC decision is Wednesday Oct 28 at 2:00 ET and it's a heavy tech earnings week. Keep size small all week.
