Launch, partnership, earnings, contract, hype. Offerings, related-party deals and compliance notices don't count.

Build it in Webull

  1. Open Webull, tap Markets, then Screeners (desktop: Screener in the left bar). Start a new custom screener.
  2. Add the filters below, then save it as Money Monday. They cover float and volume. Webull can't screen for news or past runs.
  3. From 4:00 ET Monday, sort by % Change and cross-check Top Gainers → Pre-market.
  4. For each hit, open the news tab (catalyst) and the daily chart (former runner). Grade it on all four before you trade.

Monday morning routine

  1. 4:00 Run the screener. Note every gapper with a real catalyst.
  2. 7:00 Re-run. Grade the survivors on all four checks. Keep A and B only.
  3. 8:30 Mark support on each: Friday's low, Friday's close, premarket low.
  4. 9:30 Enter on the premarket alert or mid-morning on a dip that holds support. Stop just under it.
  5. Exit At the 7–10% target, within about 3 hours, or into the close.
  6. After close Log the trade in your Trading Journal agent.

FOMC decision is Wednesday Oct 28 at 2:00 ET and it's a heavy tech earnings week. Keep size small all week.
